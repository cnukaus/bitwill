The Annual fire drill

20 minutes, four times a year

The kit includes a printable fire drill checklist. Run it every quarter. Things break quietly — a dead device, an expired 2FA, a login you forgot. The drill finds them before an emergency does.

1
Can you unlock every device?
Test PINs, passwords, and biometrics on all devices listed in your inventory.
2
Can you log into every exchange?
Including 2FA. Including recovery codes. Note any that have changed.
3
Is the envelope still sealed?
Check tamper evidence on both copies. If broken, reseal with updated forms.
4
Is your dedicated SIM still active?
Top up if needed. Check Telegram still receives messages.
5
Does your trusted person still know where their envelope is?
A quick message. Takes 30 seconds.
