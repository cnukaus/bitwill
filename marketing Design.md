The Product: What You Actually Have
Bitcoin Legacy Kit (Free Edition) — an Australian-made freemium info product helping everyday BTC holders document their holdings so family can access them. Target: non-paranoid, non-technical Aussie bitcoiners. The free offer is genuinely compelling (physical bags, $1 SIM, printable forms, no email required). The upsell is a paid letterbox service from ~$170/yr.

My Blunt Take on Channels for $20/day (~$600/month)
❌ Don't bother with paid ads yet
Not because the product is bad — it's actually well-positioned. But the freemium landing page has no email capture, no retargeting pixel visible, and no conversion tracking infrastructure. Paid traffic into a "free download, no email required" funnel burns money with nothing to optimise against. Fix that first.

✅ Free/Low-cost channels that actually fit this product
1. Stacker News — Yes, absolutely. It's the highest-signal Bitcoin community online right now. A post framed as "I built a free Bitcoin legacy kit for Aussie holders — here's why I mailed people tamper-proof bags" would do well. Don't pitch, tell the story.
2. r/BitcoinAustralia + r/AusFinance — The AusFinance subreddit (700k+ members) regularly discusses "what happens to my assets when I die" threads. You belong in those threads, not as an ad but as a genuine answer with a link.
3. Australian Bitcoin & crypto Facebook Groups — Lower signal but high volume. Groups like "Bitcoin Australia" and "Crypto Australia" have the exact "bought some BTC a few years ago" persona your page targets.
4. X/Twitter Bitcoin community — Post the "not for everyone" section as a thread. That framing is genuinely good content.
5. Local Bitcoiner meetups (Meetup.com) — Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane all have active groups. Sponsor a round of drinks (~$100) and hand out the free bags. Physical word-of-mouth is enormously credible for a physical/privacy product.

If you genuinely want to spend $20/day, spend it here
Reddit Ads targeting r/AusFinance + r/Bitcoin — this is the only paid channel I'd recommend at this stage, because the targeting is precise and the audience matches. But only after you add an email opt-in (even optional) so you have something to show for it beyond anonymous downloads.
Alternatively: spend the $600/month on postage and bags — mail kits to 50–100 Australian Bitcoin Twitter/X accounts with decent followings. The physical gift IS the marketing. That's more likely to generate organic posts than any ad.

The one thing to fix before spending anything
Add an optional email field on the gift form. "Email me when the Pro kit ships" — even 20% opt-in gives you an asset. Right now you're paying to acquire strangers who disappear after the download.
